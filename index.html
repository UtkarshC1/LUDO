📜 Classic Ludo Rules

• Yard Exit: Roll a 6 to move a pawn out of its corner yard onto your colored starting square.

• 3 Consecutive Sixes: Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits the third roll and passes the turn to the next player.

• Bonus Rolls: Earn an extra roll whenever you roll a 6, capture an opponent's pawn, or bring a pawn into the center Home triangle.

• Safe Squares (★): All 4 colored starting squares and the 4 starred track squares (8 total) are safe zones—no pawn can be captured on a ★ square.

• Captures: Landing on an opponent's pawn on a non-safe square sends that opponent's pawn back to its yard.

• Exact Home Entry: After completing 51 track steps, pawns enter their colored Home Runway and require an exact roll to reach step 56 (Home).

• Clockwise Order: Red (Bottom-Left) → Green (Top-Left) → Yellow (Top-Right) → Blue (Bottom-Right).
